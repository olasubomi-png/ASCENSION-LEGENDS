# SECTION 1 — UNIVERSE LORE

## 1.1 Creation Myth

### The Song Before Light

In the time before time, there was only the **Endless Silence** — an infinite, dimensionless void with no form, no thought, and no memory. Within this silence, however, existed a single point of impossible pressure: a compressed infinite will called the **Prima Resonance**.

For an eternity unmeasured, the Prima Resonance existed in perfect stillness. Then, without trigger or cause, it *wanted*. That singular act of desire — the universe's first conscious thought — fractured the Silence into a billion reverberations, each one carrying a fragment of intent.

These reverberations are called the **First Songs**, and from them, all existence was composed:

- The **Song of Flame** wove itself into heat, passion, and destruction — from it came the element of fire, the nature of war, and the instinct for ambition.
- The **Song of Stone** became the foundation of all solid things — from it came earth, memory, endurance, and the concept of home.
- The **Song of Wind** scattered itself through the resonating void — from it came air, freedom, speed, thought, and the nature of change.
- The **Song of Deep** pooled where the resonance was coldest — from it came water, patience, healing, secrets, and the concept of time.
- The **Song of Void** was the silence that remained between the others — from it came shadow, dreams, the unconscious mind, and the doorway between worlds.
- The **Song of Radiance** was the echo of the Prima Resonance itself — pure energy without element, without limit; the raw, crackling power that would become **Ascendance Energy** `[→ Book 1, Section 2]`.

Together, the six Songs did not merely create the physical world. They became its laws. Every creature, every stone, every storm, and every soul carries trace harmonics of all six Songs woven into its being. A warrior's strength is the Song of Stone. A mage's spell is the Song of Radiance. A shadow assassin's invisibility is the Song of Void.

### The Weavers

As the First Songs stabilized, six immense beings crystallized from the convergence points where two or more Songs intersected. These beings are called the **Weavers** — the closest thing the world has to creator-gods, though they did not consciously design what they built. They were themselves *built* by the Songs:

| Weaver | Songs | Domain | Symbol |
|--------|-------|--------|--------|
| **Solarius** | Flame + Radiance | Light, war, ambition | A sun with a sword through it |
| **Terragnost** | Stone + Deep | Earth, memory, oceans | A mountain submerged in water |
| **Velorath** | Wind + Void | Sky, change, death | A feather dissolving into shadow |
| **Crysteva** | Radiance + Stone | Magic, civilization | A gem with a heartbeat pulse |
| **Lunaketh** | Deep + Void | Dreams, time, secrets | A crescent moon over an abyss |
| **Pyraxis** | Flame + Wind | Storms, energy, war | A spiral of fire and lightning |

The Weavers did not speak to one another for the first million years. They each shaped their own corners of the nascent world in isolation. When they finally converged, they found that their creations had already begun to interact — and clash.

Rather than destroy what the others had made, they formed the **Compact of the Six**: an agreement to allow their creations to coexist, evolve, and struggle freely. The Weavers then retreated into the fabric of the world itself, becoming the metaphysical backbone of reality.

Their physical forms were never seen again — but their presence is felt in every Ascendance Energy surge, every spell, and every transformation achieved by a warrior reaching the edge of their potential.

### The First Mortals

The last act of the Weavers before their retreat was to breathe life into the world. From the mixing of all six Songs, living creatures emerged — the first mortal races. These primordial beings were raw, powerful, and driven by instinct. Over millions of years, they evolved into the civilizations that populate the world today.

## 1.2 Origin of Energy

### Ascendance Energy: The World's Lifeblood

> *Cross-reference: All Ascendance Energy gameplay mechanics are defined in Book 1, Section 2. This section covers lore only.*

**Ascendance Energy** (AE) is the direct manifestation of the Song of Radiance flowing through all living and non-living matter. It is not magic in the traditional sense — it is the fundamental force underlying all power in the world of Ascension Legends.

#### The Six Frequencies

Just as the Songs gave birth to six elemental expressions, Ascendance Energy manifests in six **Frequencies**, each corresponding to one of the original Songs:

| Frequency | Element | Color | Nature |
|-----------|---------|-------|--------|
| **Ignara** | Fire | Crimson-gold | Explosive, passionate, consuming |
| **Terris** | Earth | Deep amber | Enduring, absorptive, grounding |
| **Aeryn** | Wind | Silver-white | Swift, shifting, penetrating |
| **Aquaris** | Water | Blue-violet | Healing, deep, cleansing |
| **Umbris** | Void/Shadow | Black-purple | Concealing, draining, warping |
| **Luminara** | Radiance | Pure white-gold | Amplifying, purifying, transforming |

All player skills and abilities draw on one or more of these Frequencies. **Luminara** is unique — it is the only Frequency that can amplify others, making it the basis for Transformations `[→ Book 1, Section 7]`.

#### The Energy Cycle

Ascendance Energy is not created or destroyed — it cycles through the world in a continuous cosmic process:

```
Living beings absorb AE → Use it in action → Release it back into the world
         ↑                                              ↓
    World replenishes AE ←── Natural phenomena cycle AE back
```

Places where AE concentrates naturally are called **Resonance Points** — sacred locations where energy is dense enough to feel physically. These become the world's most contested locations: training grounds, dungeon entrances, city hubs, and the sites of the game's most powerful boss encounters.

#### Energy Corruption

When Ascendance Energy is forced, warped, or consumed faster than it can replenish, it becomes **Corrupted AE** — a dark, unstable variant that grants immense short-term power while degrading the user over time. Corrupted AE is the source of most of the game's major antagonist powers and is what transformed several legendary heroes into world-threatening villains.

## 1.5 Great Catastrophes

### The Five Great Catastrophes

These events define the transition from the Sovereign Age to the Shatter Age. Every modern culture has myths about them. Their physical evidence is visible across the landscape.

---

#### Catastrophe I: The Void Emergence

**Approximate date:** ~105,000 years ago  
**Duration:** 300 years  
**Death toll:** Estimated 500 million

**What happened:** The boundary between the Shadow Realm and the physical world began to thin at seven specific Resonance Points. From these breaches, massive Shadow Entities — beings of pure Corrupted AE and Void Frequency — poured into the physical world. They did not seek to conquer or rule; they consumed. Entire cities were absorbed. The sky turned permanently dark over affected regions for decades.

**Cause:** The Abyss Court had been conducting secret experiments to weaponize Shadow Realm energy. Their final experiment broke the barrier entirely at their capital. The Court itself was consumed first.

**Resolution:** The other five empires set aside their rivalries long enough to jointly seal six of the seven breaches. The seventh — still partially open — is now called the **Maw**, located deep in the Shadow Realm.

**World impact:** The northern continent never fully recovered. Dark energy residue still saturates the soil of Glacivast. Shadow Realm creatures remain more active there than anywhere else.

---

#### Catastrophe II: The Sundering War

**Approximate date:** ~103,000 years ago  
**Duration:** 500 years  
**Death toll:** Estimated 2 billion

**What happened:** In the chaos of the Void Emergence's aftermath, the Iron Collective blamed the Solaran Dominion for not sending aid quickly enough. The Solaran Dominion accused the Verdant Sovereignty of secretly negotiating with Shadow Realm entities. What began as political fracturing became the largest military conflict in history — three empires against three empires, with all civilian populations caught between.

**Key battles:**
- **The Siege of Golvek (Year 47 of the War)** — Solaran forces attempted to capture the Iron Collective's capital. The battle lasted 12 years and ended in mutual destruction.
- **The Burning of Liraveth (Year 211)** — Solaran fire constructs incinerated the Verdant capital's living architecture. The Sovereignty never rebuilt.
- **The Collapse of Auriheim (Year 500)** — The Dominion's own capital fell to a combined assault.

**Resolution:** None. The war simply exhausted itself. No empire was left capable of continuing. The Celestial Choir and Tide Conclave remained neutral throughout and tried unsuccessfully to broker peace.

---

#### Catastrophe III: The Ashfall

**Approximate date:** ~98,000 years ago  
**Duration:** 200 years of eruptions, ~1,000 years of winter  
**Death toll:** Estimated 800 million

**What happened:** The Sundering War's AE weaponry destabilized the planet's geological layer. A chain of volcanic eruptions began in what is now the Volcanic Lands (Section 2.3) and spread, triggering eruptions on six continents simultaneously. The ash cloud reduced sunlight by 70% for 1,000 years, causing global crop failure and famine.

**World impact:** Reshaped the landscape dramatically. The Volcanic Lands became permanently active. Several mountain ranges were born from lava fields. The Frozen Empire (Section 2.3) expanded as cooling persisted in northern regions. Volcanic rock buried the remains of the Solaran Dominion's capital, preserving it in near-perfect condition underground.

---

#### Catastrophe IV: The Silence Plague

**Approximate date:** ~95,000 years ago  
**Duration:** 600 years  
**Death toll:** Estimated 3.5 billion (40% of global population)

**What happened:** In the ash winter's starvation and desperation, a new disease emerged — the Silence Plague. It attacked the Ascendance Energy channels within living bodies, progressively shutting down the AE flow until victims could no longer use energy at all, then could no longer sustain basic biological functions. It was not contagious in the traditional sense; it spread through Resonance Point water sources that had been contaminated during the Sundering War.

**Symptoms:** Stage 1 — loss of skill access. Stage 2 — emotional flattening (the "Silence"). Stage 3 — physical deterioration. Stage 4 — death.

**Resolution:** The Tide Conclave identified the contaminated water source and developed a Aquaris-based purification technique. But by the time the cure reached affected populations, billions had already perished.

**World impact:** The Verdant Sovereignty — whose population relied entirely on shared Resonance Point water — was nearly annihilated. Modern survivors of their lineage carry a minor genetic resistance to AE channel disruption.

---

#### Catastrophe V: The Fracture

**Approximate date:** ~90,000 years ago  
**Duration:** Instantaneous event, with geological aftershocks for 10,000 years  
**Death toll:** Estimated 1 billion

**What happened:** The cumulative AE destabilization from four preceding catastrophes finally triggered a planetary-scale resonance collapse. The world's crust fractured along every major Resonance Point fault line simultaneously. Landmasses split, rose, fell, and rearranged. What had been one connected supercontinent became ten separate continents. New mountain ranges appeared overnight. Valleys flooded, becoming seas.

**World impact:** Directly responsible for the current geography of the world. The ten continents, the existence of the Sky Kingdom's floating remnants, the depth of the Ocean Kingdom's trench, and the separation of the Hidden Dimensions from the physical world — all stem from the Fracture.

## 1.8 The Modern Era

### The World Today

The Modern Era (Age VIII) began approximately 2,000 years ago with the signing of the **Accord of the Iron Table** — a peace treaty between the then-forming modern nations, establishing territorial rights, trade law, and a shared standard for AE cultivation education.

**Key features of the Modern Era:**

- **AE Technology** has merged cultivation with mechanical engineering. Cities in advanced continents (Verdantia, Ferrath) run on AE-powered infrastructure — lighting, transport, communication.
- **Twenty Factions** dominate political life. No single nation has global authority.
- **The Hidden Dimensions** were rediscovered 800 years ago, triggering a new age of exploration.
- **Racial diversity** has created hybrid cultures — most modern people carry DNA from multiple ancient races.
- **The Remembrance Compact** requires all nations to maintain Fracture Memorials, preventing historical revisionism.

Religious figures believe the Weavers are awakening. Ancient ruins are surfacing. Shadow Realm activity is at its highest in 50,000 years.

And somewhere, across every continent, new warriors are awakening with unprecedented AE capacity — the player characters.

## 1.10 Religions & Belief Systems

### Major Religions

#### The Weaver's Compact (Pantheist — Most Common)

**Followers:** ~45% of the modern world  
**Core belief:** All six Weavers are equally divine and present in the world. True spiritual growth means cultivating balance across all six Frequencies — not just one.  
**Practice:** Six-sided shrines in most cities; daily offerings of one item per Frequency; combat is considered a form of prayer when performed with purpose.  
**Views on Ascendance Energy:** AE cultivation IS worship — every skill used correctly is an act of devotion.

#### The Luminara Path (Monotheist)

**Followers:** ~25% — concentrated in Verdantia and Elorath  
**Core belief:** Only Solarius and the Song of Radiance truly exist as divine. The other Songs are derivatives or shadows of Luminara. Mortals should strive to purify themselves until only Luminara remains.  
**Practice:** White garments, daily meditation, rejection of Umbris-frequency skills (sometimes causing friction with Shadowblade users).  
**Views on Transformations:** Peak Transformations are considered moments of communion with Solarius.

#### The Deep Memory (Ancestral/Shamanist)

**Followers:** ~15% — concentrated in Terrath and Glacivast  
**Core belief:** The dead live on in the AE network. Powerful ancestors can be communicated with through ritual. History is sacred — forgetting is sin.  
**Practice:** Elaborate ancestor memorials; weekly ritual communication with ancestral spirits; oral history preservation.  
**Views on Classes:** Soulweavers and Necromancers are highly respected — they literally speak to the dead.

#### The Void Philosophy (Nihilist/Transcendentalist)

**Followers:** ~10% — spread thin globally; concentrated in the Shadow Realm's border communities  
**Core belief:** The Void is not evil — it is the truest state of existence. The other Songs are merely temporary patterns. True enlightenment is accepting nothingness.  
**Practice:** Silent meditation; voluntary AE deprivation training; "Void Walks" into the Shadow Realm.  
**Views on Shadow Realm:** Sacred space, not feared.

#### The Iron Reason (Secular/Scientific)

**Followers:** ~5% — concentrated in Ferrath (former Iron Collective territory)  
**Core belief:** The Weavers were not gods but extraordinarily powerful AE constructs that self-organized from resonance physics. AE is not spiritual — it is a natural force to be studied and engineered.  
**Practice:** Academic research, empirical AE measurement, mechanical augmentation.  
**Views on Transformations:** Physiological process, not spiritual. Can be replicated mechanically.

# SECTION 3 — FACTIONS

## Overview

Twenty major factions define the political and cultural landscape of Aethon. Players can join one faction per character (switchable with reputation cost). Faction membership provides passive bonuses, access to faction-exclusive content, and aligns the player with specific story arcs.

**Reputation System:** Each faction has 7 reputation tiers:
1. Stranger (0)
2. Acquaintance (500)
3. Recognized (2,000)
4. Trusted (5,000)
5. Honored (12,000)
6. Exalted (30,000)
7. Legend (100,000)

# SECTION 6 — TRANSFORMATIONS

## Overview

The Transformation Framework was defined in Book 1 `[→ Book 1, Section 7]`. This section expands every transformation path for each class.

### Universal Transformation Rules

> Cross-reference: All AE cost formulas for transformations use Book 1's formulas.

1. **Five Stages:** Every class has exactly 5 transformation stages.
2. **Stage Progression:** Stages unlock in order — you cannot access Stage 3 without having unlocked Stage 2.
3. **Mastery System:** Using a transformation stage accumulates Mastery EXP. At 100% Mastery, permanent passive bonuses are added.
4. **Appearance Changes:** Each stage visibly changes the character's appearance in Discord battle embeds.
5. **Transformation Aura:** Each stage emits a specific aura color tied to the class's Frequency.

# SECTION 9 — STORY MODE

## Overview

Ascension Legends features a **100-chapter linear campaign** with branching choices and **multiple endings**. The story is divided into **10 Acts** of 10 chapters each.

All player choices affect faction standing but the main story beats remain consistent. Ending variants are determined by choices in Chapters 85–100.

---

## Campaign Summary

| Act | Chapters | Title | Summary |
|-----|---------|-------|---------|
| 1 | 1–10 | The Awakening | Player is awakened by a Resonance Pulse; discovers new abilities; meets first allies and enemies |
| 2 | 11–20 | The World Stirs | Exploring the first continent; discovering the Resonance Pulse source; first encounter with Hollow Throne agents |
| 3 | 21–30 | Ancient Echoes | Entering ruins; learning about the Ancient Empires; discovering a living artifact |
| 4 | 31–40 | The Factions Move | Political story — factions react to the crisis; player must navigate conflicting loyalties |
| 5 | 41–50 | The Shadow Expands | Hollow Throne reveals itself; Shadow Realm incursions begin; first raid-level threat |
| 6 | 51–60 | Across the World | Global travel — visiting each continent; gathering allies; finding ancient weapons |
| 7 | 61–70 | The Legendary Echoes | Meeting legendary hero remnants; unlocking transformation potential; crossing into special realms |
| 8 | 71–80 | The Truth Revealed | The Voided King's history exposed; the nature of the Prima Resonance revealed; player at peak power |
| 9 | 81–90 | The Final War | The Hollow Throne makes their move; server-wide events tied to story; raid bosses as story beats |
| 10 | 91–100 | Ascension | The final confrontation; player's transformation reaches its peak; multiple endings |

## Act 1 — The Awakening (Chapters 1–10)

### Chapter 1: "The Pulse"

**Setting:** Player's home region — any continent starting area  
**Cutscene:** A blinding flash of Luminara energy erupts from underground. The player's character — previously an ordinary cultivator — absorbs an extraordinary amount of this energy. Their AE capacity quintuples overnight.  
**Objective:** Speak to the local Elder about what happened  
**Dialogue:**

> **Elder Tomas:** "I've heard stories from my grandmother's grandmother about events like this. They called them Resonance Pulses. The last one... no one alive was alive for the last one."
>
> **Player:** "What does it mean?"
>
> **Elder Tomas:** "It means the world is waking up. Whether that's a gift or a warning, I cannot say. Perhaps both."

**Gameplay:** Tutorial combat; first skills unlocked; basic movement and Discord command tutorial  
**Reward:** 500 EXP; Starter Equipment set; first 2 Active Skills

**Setting:** Regional city  
**Cutscene:** Hollow Throne agents arrive, looking for whoever absorbed the Resonance Pulse  
**Objective:** Fight off (or evade) the Hollow Throne scouts

> **Hollow Throne Scout:** "You're the one who absorbed the Pulse. Our King sends his regards. You can come with us willingly, or we can discuss alternatives."
>
> **Player:** "Who are you?"
>

**Gameplay:** First real combat encounter; Hollow Throne enemy type introduction; multiple approach choices (fight/flee/negotiate)

**Reward:** 800 EXP; 200 Coin; Hollow Throne Scout Intel (quest item)

## Act 2 Summary — "The World Stirs" (Chapters 11–20)

The player expands to the broader world — travelling between 2–3 continents, meeting faction leaders, and discovering that the Resonance Pulses are occurring globally. A rivalry subplot emerges with a memorable villain antagonist (the Hollow Throne's field general, Keth the Severed). The act ends with the player's first entry into the Spirit Realm and a shocking vision of what the Voided King is building.

**Key Chapter — Chapter 15: "The General"**

> **Keth the Severed:** "You keep fighting us. That's admirable. Wrong, but admirable. Let me explain something: we're not the enemy. We're the inevitable conclusion. The Weavers abandoned this world. The Songs are fading. The Fracture destabilized everything. We're not breaking the world — the world is already broken. Our King knows how to fix it."
>
> **Player:** "By destroying it."
>
> **Keth:** "By *transforming* it. Same thing, from a limited perspective."

---

## Act 3 Summary — "Ancient Echoes" (Chapters 21–30)

Players dive deep into the Ancient Empires' ruins. Chapter 23 features a morally complex choice: a discovered Solaran Dominion weapon could destroy a Hollow Throne stronghold — but would also harm civilians. Chapter 27 reveals the first concrete evidence of what the Voided King found in the Shadow Realm. The act ends in the Amber Compact's territory, with the discovery that the Stone Library myth is real — and something is already inside it.

---

## Act 4 Summary — "The Factions Move" (Chapters 31–40)

Political story. Every major faction has a crisis related to the Resonance Pulses. Players must help (or choose not to help) multiple factions simultaneously. The choices made here determine which factions have military resources available for Act 9's war sequence.

**Key Chapter — Chapter 35: "The Summit"**  
All faction leaders convene at Solbridge. The player is present as a guest. For the first time, the scope of the global crisis is acknowledged by everyone simultaneously. It goes badly — multiple factions leave with worse relations than they arrived.

---

## Act 5 Summary — "The Shadow Expands" (Chapters 41–50)

The Hollow Throne reveals themselves publicly. Shadow Realm incursions begin affecting the physical world — brief, alarming moments where Shadow Entities appear in daylight in major cities. Players participate in the first true raid-level content as a story beat. Chapter 49 reveals that the Voided King is attempting to reconstruct the Abyss Key — and has found three of the seven teeth.

---

## Acts 6–9 Summary (Chapters 51–90)

**Act 6 (51–60) — "Across the World":** Global travel arc; players visit each continent; each continent has a unique story beat; gather 6 legendary allies  

**Act 7 (61–70) — "The Legendary Echoes":** Encounters with legendary hero remnants (Spirit Realm); Lyssara's guidance; Kazuren's spirit trial; Rakon's impossible appearance; unlock Stage 3+ transformations  

**Act 8 (71–80) — "The Truth Revealed":** The Voided King's mortal identity revealed; his original motivation was genuinely noble; the Prima Resonance's true nature explained; the player reaches maximum power  

**Act 9 (81–90) — "The Final War":** The Hollow Throne activates the Abyss Key (partial — they have 5 teeth); the Maw partially opens; server-wide emergency event; all factions that were helped by the player in Act 4 send armies; those that weren't helped, don't

> **The Voided King:** "People die every day. I will end that. Permanently. What the void offers is not death — it is release from the cycle. The Songs created a world that destroys itself every hundred thousand years. Catastrophes. Wars. Silence Plagues. I have seen the alternative. The void is quiet. Perfect. Eternal."
>
> **Player:** "You want to end everything."
>
> **The Voided King:** "I want to save everything. The difference is perspective. The world I came from — the one I loved — was destroyed by those catastrophes. Everything I loved. The void held what remained. It holds it still. I want to bring it to everything."
>
> **Player:** "And if we choose not to?"
>
> **The Voided King:** "Then we fight. And I will lose, because I am alone and you are not. But I will not stop. I am too old to stop."

### Chapter 100: Multiple Endings

**Ending A — The Ascendant's Light (Canonical Ending)**  
Player defeats the Voided King; uses the Prima Shard to seal the Maw permanently; absorbs the shadow energy, transforming it to Luminara; world enters a new age of unprecedented peace. Player's character achieves the true Ascendant class.

**Ending B — The Voided King Redeemed**  
Player defeats the Voided King but instead of destroying him, uses Lyssara's Voice Crystal to resonate his consciousness back to humanity. He helps seal the Maw willingly. Bittersweet — he is mortal again and dies peacefully days later.

**Ending C — The Hollow Choice (Villain Path Ending)**  
If player joined Hollow Throne, player sides with the Voided King. The Maw opens fully. The world is transformed — permanently altered. New game mode unlocked: "Shadow Reign" — a post-apocalyptic alternate world.

**Ending D — The World Split (Hidden Ending)**  
Player uses the Fracture Compass inside the Maw to navigate to the Fracture Fragment. Discovers the original supercontinent before the Fracture contained a civilization that solved this exact problem. Uses their solution. World is restructured — not into pre-Fracture form but into something new. No one expected this.

## Seasonal Content Integration Rules

1. **Canonical Continuity:** Seasonal stories are canon. They advance the game's world even after the season ends.

# SECTION 17 — CONTENT PIPELINE

## Overview

This section defines how Ascension Legends creates, reviews, approves, and deploys new content. All new content must comply with the standards established in Book 1 and this document.

---

## Content Creation Process

```
IDEA → DESIGN → REVIEW → IMPLEMENT → QA → DEPLOY → MONITOR

Each stage must be completed before the next begins.
Emergency patches may skip DESIGN stage but never skip QA or REVIEW.
```

---

## How New Content is Created

### Step 1: Concept Proposal

Any design team member can submit a concept. Format:
- **What:** Clear description of new content (class, dungeon, monster, quest, item)
- **Why:** How it serves the game's philosophy `[→ Book 1, Section 1]`
- **Lore Fit:** How it fits the world of Aethon without contradiction
- **Balance Impact:** Preliminary estimate of power level
- **Effort Estimate:** Development complexity (1–5 scale)

### Step 2: Design Review

The Core Design Council reviews all proposals weekly. Criteria:
- Does it reinforce the game's core loop? `[→ Book 1, Section 2]`
- Is it completely original? (No copyrighted character/location/name references)
- Does it create interesting player decisions?
- Does it have a clear lore anchor in the existing world?

**Approval:** 3/5 council votes required for standard content; 4/5 for major content (new class, new continent)

### Step 3: Full Design Document

Approved concepts get a full design document:
- Complete stat profiles (using Book 1 formulas)
- Lore (500+ words for major content)
- Visual description for Discord embed design
- Skill interaction matrix (no broken combos)
- Accessibility check (colorblind mode; screen reader text)

---

## How Balancing is Performed

### Data Collection

Every battle generates the following telemetry:
- Class win rate per PvP bracket
- Class representation rate
- Skill usage frequency
- Skill kill contribution percentage
- Time-to-kill variance between classes
- Player satisfaction survey (optional post-battle rating)

### Balance Thresholds

| Metric | Flag Threshold | Action |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| PvP Win Rate | >55% or <45% for 2+ weeks | Balance patch |
| Class Representation | >30% or <2% of active builds | Attraction review |
| Skill Usage | >80% of all class players use same skill every fight | Skill redesign |
| Time-to-Kill | >2x average vs specific class matchup | Defensive skill review |

### Balance Philosophy

- Never make a change that removes what made a class feel good to play
- Nerfs should be narrow (one skill, not the whole class) when possible
- Buffs should address root cause (power or utility) not symptoms
- Community feedback windows: 7 days before any major balance change goes live

---

## How New Classes are Added

New classes follow a rigid 6-month pipeline:

| Month | Activity |
|-------|----------|
| Month 1 | Concept + lore design; creative review |
| Month 2 | Full stat design; skill tree; transformation design |
| Month 3 | Implementation + internal testing |
| Month 4 | Balance testing; QA; bug hunting |
| Month 5 | Beta preview (invited testers) |
| Month 6 | Launch + first 2-week monitoring patch ready |

**Class Release Cadence:** Maximum 2 new classes per season. Releases tied to seasonal story where possible.

---

## How New Regions are Introduced

Regions follow an 8-month pipeline (more complex than classes due to world-building requirements):

| Month | Activity |
|-------|----------|
| Month 1–2 | Lore development; history; faction integration |
| Month 2–3 | City design; NPC database; quest design |
| Month 3–4 | Monster database; dungeon design; boss design |
| Month 4–5 | Item/crafting integration; resource design |
| Month 5–6 | Implementation |
| Month 6–7 | QA + balance |
| Month 7–8 | Soft beta access; hard launch |
